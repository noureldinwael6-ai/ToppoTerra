# Proof Lab figure meanings

- **Masked chronology** shows NOAA statewide monthly measurements, the masked gap, adjacent estimate, selected analog estimate, and truth once revealed. Units remain physical.
- **Delay cloud** shows standardized triplets built from three consecutive known months. The selected point exposes raw dated inputs and normalized coordinates. Loop count and lifetime describe the local context.
- **Repeated archive table** shows MAE, RMSE and paired H1-minus-geometry error in physical units. Positive paired differences favor geometry. Shared observations and small samples limit generalization.
